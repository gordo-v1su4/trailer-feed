# Trailer Feed glossary

User-facing vocabulary for the portfolio. No implementation detail here.

## Take

One playable video rendition in a project, labeled **v1** through **vN**. Same idea as a **version** on a video asset in Review Room.

## Hero take

The take that represents a project on the Home hero, project poster video, and first impression when browsing. Not necessarily the newest take.

## Contact sheet

A multi-panel storyboard still (often a 3×3 grid). One image that plans many shots—not “nine separate shots” as nine videos.

## Ref image

A still used for generation context: character sheets, locations, mood boards, props. Distinct from the contact sheet grid for a take.

## Brief

Project-level creative starting text (logline, question, notes). Shown in the project drawer as the brief, not “source.”

## Portfolio

Public showcase of finished work, models, and prompts. Not a review inbox; status labels like “ready for review” are not shown on the public Home.
